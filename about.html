About Us

Our Story

A warm cup, a welcoming space, and a whole lot of heart — this is The Cozy Bean.

Who We Are

Born From a Love of Great Coffee

The Cozy Bean was built on one simple belief — that great coffee should feel like home. Nestled in the heart of Braamfontein on Melle Street, we opened our doors to create a warm, welcoming space where every cup is crafted with care and every bite is made to delight.

From our handcrafted cappuccinos and smooth lattes to our indulgent freezos and freshly baked goods, we pour love into everything we serve. Our counter is stocked fresh every morning, our beans are brewed with precision, and our team genuinely wants to make your day a little better — one cup at a time.

Whether you're starting your morning, taking a midday break, studying nearby, or catching up with a friend — The Cozy Bean is your place. Come as you are. Stay as long as you like.
